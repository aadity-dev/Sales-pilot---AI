# SalesPilot AI — Commercial Information

## Pricing
SalesPilot AI pricing is tailored to the required channels, monthly call volume, CRM and calendar integrations, security requirements, and support level. A sales representative provides a scoped proposal after understanding these requirements. No public fixed price is published in this knowledge base.

## Qualification guidance
The current qualification workflow assigns the highest budget-fit score when the stated budget is ₹50,000 or more. This is a configurable business rule, not a product price or an automatic rejection criterion.

## Demonstrations
SalesPilot AI demonstrations are 30 minutes long. The local calendar offers weekday slots at 10:00 AM, 11:00 AM, 12:00 PM, 2:00 PM, 3:00 PM, and 4:00 PM India Standard Time, subject to availability.
